Take control of a horde of ants who only want one thing: to bring delicious bread back to their anthill and grow their colony. Get bread. Spawn ants. Get more bread. Spawn more ants. Become all-powerful. Let us know how many ants you get before bricking your computer.

This game was created for the University of Waterloo Spring 2026 Game Jam, with the theme of Getaway. Originlly the ants were going to be "getting away" from some kind of anteater or something, but we ended up not having time to implement that. 

The project was created using Flutter and the Flame game framework, neither of which anyone on our team had used before. This was an interesting learning experience. We spent more time than we expected needing to implement lower-level engine features, such as move-and-slide collision, and "within a zone" consistent collision detection.
